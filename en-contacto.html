Frequently asked questions

Before you reach out

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope: a simple logo can take 1-2 weeks; a full brand identity with a manual, 3-4 weeks. I'll give you an exact estimate once we talk about your project.

How many revisions are included?

Every service includes 2 rounds of adjustments within the scope agreed at the start. Changes outside that initial scope are quoted separately.

How does payment work?

I work with a 50% deposit to reserve your project and get started, and the remaining 50% due on final delivery.

Do you work with companies outside Honduras?

Yes, I work with clients across Central America and other countries — the whole process can be handled over video call and email.

What do I need to get started?

A short conversation about your brand, your goal and your style references is enough to get started. If you already have a logo, colors or previous materials, it helps to have them on hand.

What file formats do you deliver?

You get the editable files (Illustrator or Photoshop, depending on the project) plus ready-to-use versions in PNG, JPG or PDF.

Do you handle printing too?

I design the artwork print-ready, with the correct bleed and color specs. I can coordinate the physical printing with you or directly with your trusted print shop — whichever you prefer.
